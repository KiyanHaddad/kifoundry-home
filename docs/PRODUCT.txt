KiFoundry Home — product objective

A welcoming persistent RPG home where a person can approach agents, talk naturally, show work, gather the Council and return later to continue. The project must be independently installable and usable with configured provider connections.

First complete journey: enter the town, talk to Claude, bring a script, gather Codex and selected configured specialists, discuss the opening, save a revised script beside its original, close/reopen and continue.

Council: everyone receives the same saved starting context and replies for themselves. One bounded peer-challenge stage follows, then an attributed synthesis that retains disagreements. A failed participant stays failed while useful replies survive. Council does not manufacture votes or force agreement.

Casual talk needs no project form. The town remains present. Houses identify residents, the commons gathers the group, and the studio holds shared work. Movement reflects place; actual work states come from execution.

Success requires the whole journey with real provider attribution, real saved versions, restart recovery, understandable failures, keyboard/compact-screen access and owner usability acceptance. A fixture, screenshot or passing unit test establishes only what it actually exercised.

Public release, live-provider verification and user acceptance are separate gates. Preserve existing user data during upgrades and explicit migrations.
